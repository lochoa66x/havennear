# HavenNear Project Charter

## Purpose

HavenNear exists to help people in Canada find nearby shelter and essential
services with less unnecessary travel, uncertainty, and overcrowding.

The original idea belongs to Razan. The project is dedicated:

> **For Razan.**

## Non-negotiable principles

### Free access

People seeking help must never be charged to search, call, view public shelter
information, or use HavenNear's connection features.

### No profit from suffering

HavenNear must not be used to sell access, leads, placement priority, personal
information, advertising targeted at people in crisis, or any other product
whose value comes from restricting or exploiting access to help.

Fair compensation for real labour is different from profiting from suffering.
Shelter workers, technical staff, translators, accessibility specialists,
security professionals, and other contributors may be paid reasonable
compensation for their work by an otherwise permitted organization.

### Privacy by design

HavenNear is a directory and connection service, not a case-management system.
It must not collect or maintain shelter guest registrations, case notes, health
details, dates of birth, government identifiers, immigration status, or intake
records.

Shelters remain responsible for their own lawful intake and internal records.

### Dignity and accessibility

The public experience should be understandable under stress, usable on older
phones and slow connections, and accessible without creating an account.
Language must be direct, respectful, and non-stigmatizing.

### Verified public information

Community suggestions and imported records remain private until a human reviewer
checks an authoritative source. Confidential shelter locations must never be
published. Availability must show its age and expire when it is no longer
reliable.

### No preferential placement

HavenNear must not sell ranking, bury shelters for commercial reasons, or let
payments influence which option appears closest. Search ordering should serve
the person seeking help.

## Governance

Material changes to these principles, the licence, privacy boundaries, data
collection, commercial-use restrictions, shelter ranking, or the Razan
attribution require explicit maintainer review and must not be merged as routine
technical changes.

Contributors may propose improvements, but no contribution overrides this
charter merely by being accepted into the codebase.

## Name and attribution

The HavenNear name, visual identity, and Razan attribution must not be used to
imply endorsement of an unrelated service or organization.

The project may eventually be transferred to a qualified charitable,
public-health, public-safety, or government institution that agrees to preserve
this charter, the privacy boundary, free access, and the required attribution.
