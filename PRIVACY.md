# HavenNear Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

## Our privacy promise

HavenNear is designed to help people find shelters without requiring an account
or building a profile about them. We collect the least information needed to
operate a trustworthy public directory.

## People seeking shelter

HavenNear does not ask a person seeking help for:

- a name, email address, telephone number, or account;
- a date of birth, government identifier, immigration status, or health record;
- case notes, intake answers, or shelter history; or
- payment information.

When a browser uses location to calculate distance, coordinates are intended to
remain on that device. The current application does not send precise seeker
coordinates to HavenNear's shelter API or store them in the directory database.

Searches do not reserve a bed or register a person with a shelter. A shelter may
collect information through its own intake process under its own policies and
legal responsibilities. HavenNear does not control or receive those records.

## Public shelter information

The public directory may contain information intended for public use, including:

- shelter and organization names;
- public or safely generalized locations;
- public telephone numbers and websites;
- hours, intake instructions, eligibility, accessibility, and services;
- time-sensitive availability supplied by participating shelters; and
- source and verification dates.

Confidential shelter addresses must not be published.

## Community corrections

The correction form does not request the submitter's identity or contact
information. A correction may include the selected shelter, correction type,
description, optional public source link, submission time, review status, and a
private reviewer note.

Submitters must not include information about a shelter guest or person seeking
help. Corrections are never applied automatically.

## Shelter and operator accounts

Authorized shelter representatives and directory operators may be identified by
their work email or authentication identity. HavenNear may store:

- organization and professional contact details;
- participation request and approval status;
- assigned shelter access;
- administrative actions and timestamps; and
- availability updates.

This information is used for authorization, directory integrity, and audit
purposes. It is not displayed as seeker information.

## Technical data

Hosting and security providers may process ordinary request data such as IP
address, browser information, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to deliver and
protect the service. HavenNear should not add advertising trackers, data brokers,
cross-site behavioural profiling, or sale of usage data.

Operational logs should avoid request bodies and precise location. Retention
should be limited to the shortest period reasonably needed for reliability,
security, abuse prevention, and legal obligations.

## Sharing

HavenNear does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by
infrastructure providers strictly to host, secure, and operate the service, or
disclosed when legally required.

HavenNear does not share a person's search activity with shelters as an intake
record.

## Retention and correction

Public shelter facts are retained while useful and are reviewed for accuracy.
Administrative records are retained only as needed for authorization, audit,
security, or legal obligations. Invalid or unnecessary records should be
corrected, anonymized, or deleted.

Until a dedicated privacy contact is established, privacy requests should be
sent privately to the repository owner or current HavenNear operator. Do not put
personal information in a public GitHub issue.

## Changes

Changes that introduce seeker accounts, server-side precise-location storage,
advertising, analytics profiling, case management, or new categories of
personal information require an explicit privacy review and a visible update to
this policy before release.
