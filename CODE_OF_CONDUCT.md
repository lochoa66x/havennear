# HavenNear Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free community.
People experiencing homelessness are not data points, marketing opportunities,
or examples for ridicule. Their dignity and safety come first.

## Expected behaviour

- Use respectful, person-centred language.
- Listen to people with lived experience and frontline expertise.
- Protect privacy and confidential locations.
- Give constructive, specific technical feedback.
- Credit sources, contributors, and the Razan attribution.
- Design for stress, disability, low bandwidth, and older devices.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and potential commercial benefit.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or demeaning language;
- publishing personal, case, health, intake, or confidential-location data;
- attempting to identify or contact shelter residents through project data;
- exploitative advertising, lead generation, pay-to-rank, or data brokerage;
- misrepresenting unverified availability as a guaranteed placement;
- retaliation against good-faith safety, privacy, or security reports; or
- using the HavenNear name to imply endorsement without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, revoke access, or
refer serious safety concerns to the appropriate platform or authority. Reports
should be made privately to the repository owner or current HavenNear operator.
Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should prioritize the safety and dignity of people seeking
shelter and be documented privately when confidentiality is required.
